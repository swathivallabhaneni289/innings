<objective>
Seed the Vault with about 50 real classic matches across eras, formats and the women's game, and fact-check every one before it ships.

Purpose: The Vault is what fans open on days with no cricket, and the archive is only worth anything if it is right. A wrong date or scoreline in a "classic" undermines the whole app.
Output: `internal/sim/classics.json` and `docs/VAULT_SOURCES.md`.
</objective>

<context>
@docs/PLAN.md
@internal/sim/classics.go
@src/domain/types.ts
</context>

<tasks>

<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 1: Draft the seed</name>
  <files>internal/sim/classics.json</files>
  <action>Write one `ClassicSeed` per match: id, date (first day for Tests), competition id and name, stage, format, gender, team ids from the sim's list, venue, winner (null for a tie or draw), result text, innings in batting order, scoreline, official Player of the Match where one was named, a headline of at most 60 characters, collections, era and a hype score from 1 to 5. Cover every collection, including the women's game and tied matches decided by a Super Over.</action>
  <acceptance_criteria>
    - The file parses with `LoadClassicSeeds`
    - Each of the five collections has at least one match
  </acceptance_criteria>
</task>

<task type="checkpoint:verify" gate="blocking">
  <name>Task 2: Triple fact-check</name>
  <files>internal/sim/classics.json, docs/VAULT_SOURCES.md</files>
  <action>Three checker agents, working independently, each verify every entry against web sources (date, venue, teams, result, scoreline, Player of the Match). An entry is corrected only when the sources agree, and dropped when it cannot be verified. Record one source per match in `docs/VAULT_SOURCES.md`.</action>
  <acceptance_criteria>
    - Every entry in `classics.json` has a row in `docs/VAULT_SOURCES.md`
    - The checkers' tallies (confirmed, corrected, dropped) are recorded in the summary
  </acceptance_criteria>
</task>

</tasks>
